NEED TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Helpful details about ClipLaunch.

What is ClipLaunch?

ClipLaunch is intended to help creators prepare video files, captions, hashtags, and available privacy settings for publishing to their own TikTok accounts.

Do I need my own TikTok account?

Yes. You need an eligible account and must authorize the connection through the supported TikTok authentication process.

Can I choose who sees my video?

Available audience options depend on TikTok's rules, your account, and the permissions made available to the app.

Does ClipLaunch guarantee publication?

No. Publishing can depend on API availability, account eligibility, content requirements, network conditions, and platform restrictions.

How is my information handled?

Read our Privacy Policy for information about data processing, retention, security, and deletion requests.
