Privacy Policy

How ClipLaunch handles information when you use our video publishing tool.

Effective date: October 12, 2026

ClipLaunch (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, disclosed, and protected when you use ClipLaunch to prepare and publish video content to your TikTok account.

This policy must reflect the app’s actual technical behavior. Before making this page public, confirm and revise the descriptions below to match where your app processes files, whether it stores tokens or logs, and which service providers it uses.

1. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use and the permissions you grant, ClipLaunch may handle:

  • TikTok account information: identifiers and profile information made available through TikTok’s authorization and APIs.
  • Authorization information: access tokens and related credentials needed for an authorized connection. These must be protected and handled according to applicable requirements.
  • Video and post details: video files you select, captions, hashtags, and privacy or audience settings you choose.
  • Technical information: diagnostic logs, error details, and basic device or application information, if collected by the app or its infrastructure.
  • Support communications: information you provide when contacting us for help.

The precise information processed depends on the implemented features and the TikTok permissions enabled in your version of ClipLaunch.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used as reasonably necessary to connect your account through TikTok’s authorization process; prepare and submit videos and associated post details when you request publication; show publication progress or errors; maintain and secure the app; respond to support requests; and comply with legal obligations.

ClipLaunch does not sell personal information. Do not use this statement unless it accurately reflects your app and business practices.

3. Video Files and Publishing

You choose which videos and captions to use. When you instruct ClipLaunch to publish content, relevant information is transmitted to TikTok using the authorized integration.

Video files may be processed locally on your computer or transmitted to our or a service provider’s systems, depending on the app’s technical design. The operator must accurately specify where files are processed, whether temporary copies are created, and when those copies are deleted.

4. TikTok and Third-Party Services

ClipLaunch uses TikTok developer services for supported account and publishing features. TikTok processes information under its own terms and privacy policies.

If ClipLaunch uses hosting, diagnostics, or other service providers, those providers may process information necessary to perform their services and should be subject to appropriate safeguards. We do not disclose personal information to other parties except as described in this policy, with your direction or consent, or as required or permitted by law.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer period is required by law. Your actual retention and deletion practices should be described here before launch.

To request deletion of information associated with ClipLaunch, including applicable account data or stored authorization information, email earleanm.rider@gmail.com with the subject “ClipLaunch Data Deletion Request.” We may request reasonable information to verify your request.

We will process verified requests within the time required by applicable law and explain any information that must be retained. Revoking access through TikTok may stop future authorized access, but does not necessarily delete information already held by ClipLaunch or TikTok. You may need to contact TikTok separately regarding information it holds.

6. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards should be used to protect the information handled by ClipLaunch. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Access tokens and credentials must not be exposed in public code, browser logs, or public repositories.

7. Your Choices and Rights

You can choose what content to submit and whether to authorize the TikTok connection. You can stop using ClipLaunch and request deletion as described above. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, restrict, or delete personal information, or object to certain processing.

You can manage or revoke connected-app permissions through the options TikTok provides. Revocation may prevent some ClipLaunch features from working.

8. Children’s Privacy

ClipLaunch is not intended for children who are not permitted to use the service under applicable law or TikTok’s age requirements. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided information improperly, contact us so we can review the matter.

9. International Data Transfers

Information may be processed in countries other than your own if our actual service providers or infrastructure operate internationally. Where required, appropriate legal safeguards should be used for international transfers.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as ClipLaunch evolves or legal requirements change. We will publish the revised policy on this page and update the effective date. Where legally required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent.
